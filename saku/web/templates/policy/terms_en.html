About the beta

Saku is a personal research and development project in a closed beta. Access is given by hand, can be withdrawn, and the service can change or stop at any time. It is offered free of charge and without any guarantee.

What Saku is and is not

  • Saku shows information computed from data you provide. It does not check whether that data is complete or correct.
  • Saku is not financial, investment, tax, lending or insurance advice. Decisions about your money remain yours.
  • Saku cannot move money, make payments or access your bank. It only reads files and messages you send it.

Your part

  • Upload only your own statements, or statements you are allowed to use.
  • Never enter internet banking passwords, PINs or OTPs, full account numbers or card numbers.
  • Keep access to your email account safe. Anyone who can read it can sign in as you.
  • Do not try to reach other users' data, overload the service or probe it for weaknesses without permission. If you find a security problem, please report it to the contact in the privacy policy.

Ending access

You can stop using Saku and ask for your data to be deleted at any time. The owner may end a beta account, for example to protect the service or other users, and will try to give notice first.
