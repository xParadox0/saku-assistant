Who runs Saku

Saku is a personal research and development project run by one person, Yona Surya, from Indonesia. It is not a bank, not a payment provider and not a licensed financial adviser. It is in a closed beta.

Contact for anything in this policy: {{ contact }}.

What Saku stores

  • Your email address, verified by Cloudflare Access when you sign in with a one-time code.
  • Your beta request: the name you choose to show, an optional note, the language you used, the version of this policy you accepted and when.
  • If your request is approved: the accounts you add (a name, the type, the provider and at most the last 4 digits of a number) and the transactions read from statements you upload or messages you send to the Saku bot.
  • If you connect Telegram: your numeric Telegram user ID. Not your phone number and not your username.

What Saku never stores

  • Internet banking usernames, passwords, PINs or OTPs. Saku never asks for them.
  • Full account or card numbers. Descriptions are cleaned of account numbers, phone numbers and email addresses before they are saved.
  • The statement files themselves. A file is read, then deleted. Only a fingerprint (a SHA-256 hash) is kept, so the same file is not imported twice.

Why

To show you your own money picture across accounts, and to run the beta: decide who gets access and contact you about your account. Saku does not use your data for advertising, does not build a credit score and does not sell or share your data.

Where the data is

On a server operated by the project owner in Indonesia. Traffic reaches it through Cloudflare, which handles the sign-in and the encrypted connection. Telegram processes the messages you send to the bot. If a feature later sends text to an AI model provider, that text is cleaned of personal details first, and this policy will be updated before that happens.

How long

  • A request that is not approved: until you ask for deletion, and at most 90 days after the decision.
  • Account and transaction data: until you delete it or ask for deletion.
  • Backups may keep deleted data for a short period before they are overwritten.

Your rights

Under Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law (UU No. 27 of 2022) you may ask for access to your data, a copy of it, correction, deletion, and to withdraw consent. Send the request to the contact above. Export and delete buttons are added to the dashboard before the beta accepts other users.

Security

Sign-in goes through Cloudflare Access. The server has no open port to the internet. Each user's data is separated in the database and every query is filtered by the user it belongs to. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data, you will be told.

Changes

A new version of this policy gets a new version date. If a change affects how your data is used, you will be asked to accept it again.
